Privacy Policy for Podbbang Unofficial

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Information and storage

Podbbang Unofficial does not require an account. Favorites, the listening queue, recently opened channels, playback progress, and app preferences are saved in local browser storage on your device. This data is not sent to the app developer and is not synced between devices.

Network requests

To show podcast listings and search results, the app sends requests to its API proxy and Podbbang services. Search terms and channel identifiers needed for those requests may be included. Audio playback connects to the media service that hosts each episode. These service providers may process request information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Sharing and retention

The app developer does not operate an account service, collect the local library, or sell personal information. Local library and playback data remain on your device until you clear the app's site data or remove them in the app.

Third-party services

This is an unofficial application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Podbbang. Please refer to Podbbang's privacy policy for information about its services.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact Piano8283 Studio through the app's publisher support page.
